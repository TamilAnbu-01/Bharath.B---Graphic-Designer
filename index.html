(01) About

I'm Bharath — a graphic designer turning briefs into campaigns people stop scrolling for. Today I build the visual world of international finance expos across Dubai, Mumbai, Abu Dhabi, Hong Kong and Latin America.

Over six years I've moved from Australian caravan dealers and property developers at Web Logix to multi-market event brands at Trasol Technology & Events. Each campaign runs from speaker announcements and sponsor reveals to emailers, booth sales and countdowns, and often in two languages.

I work remotely as part of a small design team in Erode, India. My work sits where composition meets conversion. I pair Adobe craft with SEO awareness and AI tools like Midjourney, Firefly and NanoBanana, so the work moves fast and still looks considered.
